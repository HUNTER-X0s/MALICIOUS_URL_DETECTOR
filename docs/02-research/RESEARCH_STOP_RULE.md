# Research stopping rule

Research should be broad enough to support a decision, not endless. Stop a research thread when: (1) the question and decision are explicit; (2) current primary sources and independent/contrary evidence have been checked; (3) material licensing/privacy/security constraints are known; (4) remaining uncertainty is documented; and (5) a prototype or decision can answer the next question more efficiently. Re-open research when new evidence, threat shifts, provider terms or user feedback changes assumptions.
