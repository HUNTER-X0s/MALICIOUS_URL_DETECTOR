# Personas and jobs to be validated

- Consumer: decide whether to open a link received in a message.
- Student/family: understand suspicious links without cybersecurity expertise.
- SMB operator: protect staff from phishing and impersonation.
- Security analyst: triage links with evidence, provenance and bulk workflows.
- Developer: integrate URL checks with predictable API semantics.
- Enterprise administrator: manage policies, tenants, audit and integrations.

For each persona validate trust needs, accessibility, device/browser, workflow, privacy expectation, latency tolerance, false-positive cost and willingness to pay. Avoid treating personas as evidence of demand.
