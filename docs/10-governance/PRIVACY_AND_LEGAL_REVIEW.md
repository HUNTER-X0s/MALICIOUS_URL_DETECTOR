# Privacy and legal governance

This document is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Identify operating jurisdictions, customer locations, data subjects, data categories, purposes, legal bases, processors, subprocessors, transfers, retention, data subject rights, breach obligations and sector requirements. Obtain qualified counsel before launch in each relevant jurisdiction.

Treat submitted URLs as potentially sensitive because they may carry credentials, session tokens, medical/financial identifiers, internal hostnames or private document IDs. Minimize collection, default-redact, encrypt, access-limit and define short retention. Establish DPA/vendor agreements, privacy policy, terms, acceptable-use policy, abuse policy, vulnerability disclosure policy and incident notification process.
