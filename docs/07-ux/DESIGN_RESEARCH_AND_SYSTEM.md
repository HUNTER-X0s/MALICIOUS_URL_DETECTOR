# UI/UX research and design system brief

## Research before style selection
Study direct malicious-link scanners, browser security warnings, enterprise SOC tools, security APIs, high-quality consumer SaaS and high-trust financial/identity products. Record actual flows, screenshots where permitted, navigation, typography, information density, empty/loading/error states, evidence presentation, accessibility, responsiveness, motion and interaction behavior.

## Design experiments
Explore distinct directions, for example:
- Precision editorial: restrained typography, strong hierarchy, clear evidence.
- Modern technical workspace: compact status surfaces, tables and timelines for analysts.
- Calm consumer protection: plain-language guidance and strong action clarity.
- Selective material depth/glass effects for secondary surfaces only.

Do not choose skeuomorphism, glassmorphism, neumorphism, brutalism, bento grids or “liquid glass” merely because they are fashionable. Use a coherent system appropriate to the task and performance/accessibility budget. Do not clone Google/Meta product UI, branding or assets.

## Required deliverables
Design audit, competitor matrix, 2-3 prototype directions, user test, selected design rationale, design tokens, typography/color/spacing, component library, state catalogue, motion guidelines, responsive specs and accessibility audit.

## Breakpoint coverage
Test at least 320px width; small/large phones; portrait/landscape tablets; laptops; 1440p+ desktops; ultrawide; zoom; text scaling; touch; reduced motion; light/dark if supported. Breakpoints should be content-driven, not tied only to device labels.

## Website and extension are different contexts
The public website can be expressive and deep. The extension must be compact, fast, accessible, low-distraction and trustworthy. Share tokens/components where appropriate, not rigid layouts.
