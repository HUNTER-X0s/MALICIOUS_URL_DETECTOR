# Vision and differentiation hypotheses

Potential differentiation to validate rather than assume:
- Clear separation of verdict, confidence, evidence, freshness and recommended action.
- Honest `UNKNOWN` when evidence is insufficient or stale.
- Low-permission, privacy-respecting browser protection.
- Fast local-first checks plus bounded server intelligence.
- Explainable brand impersonation, IDN/confusable and redirect-chain signals.
- Signed verdict/evidence provenance if key management, revocation and verification justify complexity.
- Community and analyst feedback with poisoning resistance, moderation and appeal.
- Enterprise policy, audit and integration capabilities based on validated customer discovery.

Do not assume blockchain, LLMs, microservices, graph databases or a particular ML model are required. Each needs a problem statement, measurable benefit, threat analysis, alternatives, cost and rollback plan.
