# Research evidence policy

For every important external proposition include a citation that a reader can open. A useful citation record contains source title, publisher, URL, date published/updated (if available), date accessed, relevant section, what the source establishes, what it does not establish, and why it matters to URLSentinel. Record conflicting sources and uncertainty. Do not fabricate a publication date or cite search result snippets as if the underlying page was read. Recheck changing API/provider rules immediately before implementation/release.
