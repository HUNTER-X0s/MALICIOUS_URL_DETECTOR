# Accuracy and claims policy

No claims such as “detects every malicious URL”, “100% safe”, “zero false positives”, “unhackable”, “military-grade”, “world's number one”, certified, independently audited or guaranteed uptime unless independently substantiated and current. Distinguish aspirational goals from verified properties. Public trust copy should be understandable, supportable and dated where time-sensitive. Never fake metrics, customer logos, reviews, scans, certifications, uptime or detection outcomes.
