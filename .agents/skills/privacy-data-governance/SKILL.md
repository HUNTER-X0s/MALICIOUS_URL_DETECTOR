# Privacy and governance

Inventory data and purpose; identify sensitive URL components; minimize collection and retention; set access controls, encryption, deletion, export, audit and backup expiry. Avoid logging full query strings, fragments, credentials, tokens or submitted URLs by default. Assess consent/legal basis, processor roles, cross-border transfers and contractual obligations. Provide transparent user controls. Research current jurisdictional law with qualified review. Do not treat this skill as legal advice.
