# UI/UX and competitor research

Identify direct URL scanners, anti-phishing products, browser safety features, endpoint/email security suites, threat intelligence platforms and strong consumer SaaS references. Compare product flows, trust language, evidence display, speed, onboarding, accessibility, extension interaction, privacy controls and failure states. Research design patterns and typography/color/motion systems. Do not copy protected assets or misleading UI. Create a comparative matrix and 2-3 divergent prototype directions; conduct usability tests and document rationale before locking a design system.
