# Trust UX and product design

Audit leading security products and high-quality SaaS, enterprise consoles and accessibility references. Capture dated screenshots only where terms permit; record what is useful and what should not be copied. Explore visual directions (editorial minimalism, precise data visualization, restrained depth/material effects, bento layouts, etc.) through prototypes and usability tests. Choose a coherent design language for the job; do not combine trends indiscriminately or imitate Google/Meta. Make primary scan action obvious; communicate verdict, confidence, evidence, freshness, limitations and next steps. Distinguish malicious, suspicious, inconclusive, unavailable and known benign. Do not use green checkmarks, shields or celebratory language to imply certainty. Test trust comprehension with users.
