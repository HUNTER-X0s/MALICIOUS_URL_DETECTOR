# Privacy and data governance

- URLs can contain credentials, reset tokens, personal data and internal hostnames; treat as sensitive.
- Data collection must be purpose-limited, disclosed, minimized, access-controlled and retained only as needed.
- No cross-provider sharing without legal basis, contract and disclosure. Redact secrets and support applicable privacy rights.
- Research current law per jurisdiction; this package is not legal advice.
