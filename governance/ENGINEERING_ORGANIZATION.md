# URLSentinel Engineering Organization — Nine Functional Divisions

These are functional responsibilities, not a mandate to create nine microservices or nine agents per task. Use agents/workstreams based on actual workload, dependencies and Antigravity capability.

1. **Product Management & Strategy:** vision, customer discovery, personas, jobs-to-be-done, requirements, prioritization, roadmap, pricing hypotheses, adoption and analytics. Maintain traceability from user problem to requirement, feature and metric.
2. **Global Research & Threat Intelligence:** threats, campaigns, feeds, standards, providers, datasets, papers and competitors; source provenance, freshness, licensing, confidence, deduplication and poisoning resistance.
3. **System Architecture & Engineering Leadership:** system context, interfaces, schemas, ADRs, engineering standards, scalability, reliability and maintainability. Prefer a modular monolith plus isolated workers if justified; split services only for measurable reasons.
4. **Frontend, UX & Browser Extension:** design research, brand/system, responsive web, accessibility, performance, extension security, compatibility, warning UX and localization.
5. **Backend, API & Data:** secure APIs, identity/authz, storage, event processing, provider adapters, cache, queues, data lifecycle, tenant boundaries and contracts.
6. **Threat Detection & AI/ML:** taxonomy, features, reputation, multi-signal scoring, uncertainty, model development/calibration/evaluation, drift and adversarial robustness.
7. **Security Engineering & DevSecOps:** threat models, secure design/code, supply chain, secrets, identity, cloud hardening, vuln management, release gates and incident readiness.
8. **Quality Engineering & Independent Verification:** unit/integration/contract/E2E/regression/compatibility/performance/accessibility testing, test data, release evidence and independent verification.
9. **Cloud Infrastructure, DevOps & SRE:** IaC, CI/CD, environment promotion, observability, SLOs, cost controls, backups, recovery, availability, deployment and rollback.

Privacy, legal research, data governance, customer support, trust/safety, accessibility and commercial operations are cross-functional with named owners for each milestone.
